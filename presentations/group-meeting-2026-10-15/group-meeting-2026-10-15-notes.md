## 1. LESTO project update
Introduce the work as an extension of Bojan Ničeno's frozen-flow species solver. The title graphic is a conceptual cooling-column diagram, not a simulation field. The aim is to predict transport, chemical redistribution and wall deposition of Pb–Bi–I species while preserving material.

## 2. Project status
The six numbers describe different dimensions of the work; they must not be added together. The 19 production cases rerun the 15 original baselines and four silica-coordinate variants. They use 41,472 cells and limitedLinear advection. The 64 input-sensitivity follow-ups remain on 18,432 cells with upwind advection. There are nine reacting gases in addition to helium, and four condensed inventories. The 18 K result is the onset RMS error over five Lobresco experiments, simulated on two separate meshes (22,896 and 10,176 cells). Numerical PASS is not physical validation.

## 3. Bojan's baseline
Attribution is documented in section 31 of applications/rhoFixedFlowFoam/doc/phase-change-plan.md. Bojan's tag 0.1 already includes conservative gas-to-solid mock coupling: an implicit gas sink supplies the solid through its realised value, with per-species conservation checks. His main branch also includes an xGEMS probe and startup demonstration. Do not describe the baseline as non-conservative or as lacking thermochemistry entirely. Your additions create a separate physical phase-change path, richer inventories, gas equilibrium, element accounting and a verification/experimental programme. ## 4. Reversible wall exchange
HKS means Hertz–Knudsen–Schrage. Positive j is wall-to-gas mass flux, in kg m^-2 s^-1. Equilibrium pressure is selected from thermodynamic tables at wall temperature. The cell-centre partial pressure uses chi = rho R T/M. Adding the half-cell diffusion resistance gives the effective wall conductance. The wall and gas use the same realised transfer; bounds stop a bare wall from evaporating. The diagram is conceptual. This removes an arbitrary volumetric wall-layer thickness from the physical formulation, but does not remove mesh error. In these column cases, the sensitivity evidence supports diffusion-limited exchange. See manuscript/sections/03-model.tex and solvePairedGasSpecies.H.

## 5. Multispecies thermochemistry
Gases: Pb, PbI, PbI2, Bi, Bi2, BiI, BiI3, I and I2. Condensed stores: PbI2, BiI3, Pb and Bi. Bi and Bi2 exchange with the same Bi inventory, preventing double counting. The displayed wall reaction is an implemented forward-only channel, not an experimentally qualified rate law. The reported column campaign uses the specialised ideal-gas element-potential kernel, not GEMS at every cell. GEMS bridge modes are separate capabilities. Carrier velocity, density and temperature remain prescribed; aerosol pathways, substrate reactions and thermal feedback are outside the current model.

## 6. Numerical verification
The chart compares successive meshes for the difficult steel Q2 shifted-BiI case. Iodine profile change is the sum of absolute bin differences divided by the reference total iodine. limitedLinear changes are 15.35%, 8.95% and 1.98%; upwind changes are 10.47%, 10.58% and 2.13%. The last comparison is 41,472 to 93,312 cells. This does not prove upwind is more accurate: at equal mesh it differs from limitedLinear by 20.8%, 14.1%, 11.3% and 7.7%. Time-step halving changes tested profiles by at most 0.323%; eight rather than three correctors changes the three completed full-run checks by at most 0.021%. These maxima are over different tested subsets, not a universal uncertainty budget. The worst production raw element residue is 1.41e-12 relative to supplied material. Apparent convergence orders are inconsistent, so no formal GCI is claimed. The fourth, shifted-BiI corrector check is not used.

## 7. Published PbI2 benchmark
Bars show onset minus experiment, in kelvin. Labels are standard flow in mL/min / duration in seconds. Our errors are +26, +19, +16, -11 and -14 K. Published temperature-model errors are -39, -10, -1, -60 and -30 K; published vapour-pressure-model errors are +27, +37, +34, -50 and -15 K. RMS values round to 18, 35 and 35 K. Source: manuscript/generated/lobresco-table.tex, based on Lobresco et al. (2026), Table 2 and run/035. This is an independent comparison against published curves, not new matched-input T-Flows code-to-code verification. The onset criterion is 1% of peak deposit; for 5 mL/min it is applied downstream of the sample because upstream back diffusion creates a separate small deposit. Three errors exceed the stated ±15 K uncertainty. Peaks remain 1–4 cm upstream; shutdown, sample withdrawal and the boat are absent. The 0.33–0.36 source-region mole fraction breaches the dilute passive-carrier assumption. Therefore describe the onset result as improvement on this metric, not overall validation or universal superiority.

## 8. Thermodynamic sensitivity
Both bars use production steel Q2 at 1% metal saturation. Q2 prescribes elemental iodine release inferred from deposits and an assumed metal loading; it is not a fully predicted source. The fraction counts iodine in deposited BiI3 divided by iodine in deposited PbI2 plus BiI3. It excludes KI and other iodine pathways. The 94.58% versus 2.01% contrast results from adding 29 kJ/mol to BiI(g) standard Gibbs energy, consistently in formation and reactive-wall constants. It is a large hypothetical perturbation. Matched-resolution comparisons retain more than 92.5 percentage points separation, establishing robustness of sensitivity, not which data branch is correct.

## 9. Silica temperature registration
Original Q1 production peaks occur at 56/63 cm; measured maxima are at 51/59 cm. Published temperature labels and the reconstructed curve disagree by approximately 63 and 108 K at the reported positions. The two shifts derive from those published inputs: temperature is shifted upstream by 4.096 or 4.653 cm, without moving the measured iodine scan. Q1 overlap increases from 6.13% to 77.80% or 73.26%. Overlap is the shared area of normalised profiles, not fraction of released iodine recovered. The shifted peaks are 51/59 and 51/58 cm. These are hypotheses, not corrections to experimental truth. Their ranking reverses between upwind and limitedLinear, so neither is selected. Ask for original thermocouple data and coordinate registration.

## 10. Next steps

Discuss priorities: recover temperature coordinates, pressure/flow reference and release history; qualify BiI thermodynamics; extend spatial checks to other cases; represent shutdown/cooling and the source region.
