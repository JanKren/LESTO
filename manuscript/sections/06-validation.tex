\section{Conditional comparison with deposition measurements}
\label{sec:comparison}
\subsection{Comparison measures}
The primary comparison uses reconstructed relative iodine yields per
1 cm bin. Let $e_b$ be the experimental percentage and let
\begin{equation}
 m_b=(100-y_{\rm KI})\frac{a_b}{\sum_{\rm full\ column}a_b}
 \label{eq:yield}
\end{equation}
be the modelled percentage, with the stated excluded KI yield
$y_{\rm KI}=0$, 6.6 or 7.0. Over the common scanned bins $\mathcal B$,
we report the raw discrepancy
\begin{equation}
 L_{\rm raw}=\sum_{b\in\mathcal B}|m_b-e_b|
 \quad\text{(percentage points)}.
 \label{eq:raw-l1}
\end{equation}
This preserves the experimental digitisation sum and the model's
explicit KI exclusion. It is neither an absolute deposited-mass error
nor a percentage relative to an experimental uncertainty.

A secondary shape diagnostic sets
$\widehat m_b=m_b/\sum_{\mathcal B}m_b$ and
$\widehat e_b=e_b/\sum_{\mathcal B}e_b$, and calculates
\begin{equation}
 O=100\sum_{b\in\mathcal B}\min(\widehat m_b,\widehat e_b).
 \label{eq:overlap}
\end{equation}
Overlap $O$ is conditional on the common scanned range and discards
capture and total-yield differences. The experimental normalisation
still contains KI. It is a descriptive shape measure, not a validation
probability or a fitted goodness-of-fit statistic.

\subsection{Q1 peaks and complete profiles}
\Cref{tab:peaks} compares the five available Q1 iodide maxima with the
printed peak temperatures. All calculated peak temperatures lie within
the stated temperature ranges. Pure PbI$_2$ is near the low-temperature
edge of its range, and its binned maximum changes under refinement
as described above. Steel LBE places both iodide maxima at the measured
positions, while silica LBE places PbI$_2$ and BiI$_3$ respectively
5 and 4 cm downstream. Agreement with a printed temperature is therefore
not sufficient to establish the correct spatial location.

\Cref{fig:baseline} retains the full profile comparison. The raw
$L_{\rm raw}$ values are 53.90, 53.45 and 181.84 percentage points for
pure PbI$_2$, steel LBE and silica LBE. Corresponding normalised overlaps
are 72.93\%, 71.88\% and 7.20\%. The silica case therefore has a
large profile discrepancy even though both printed temperature ranges
contain its calculated maxima.

The Q1 steel BiI$_3$ share of deposited iodide iodine is 65.34\%, close
to the measured iodide-only share of $61.3/(61.3+32.1)=65.63\%$.
For silica the corresponding values are 28.68\% and
$27/(27+66)=29.03\%$. These agreements are substantially conditioned
by the source masses inferred from those same deposits. They cannot
serve as independent validation of gas speciation or LBE release.
Likewise, source-driven calculations capture 91.27--98.20\% of supplied
iodine on the wall by 60 s, but that does not establish experimental
three-hour capture: the remaining inventory, release history and
post-release cooling are different questions.
