%==============================================================================
\section{Reference experiments: thermosublimatography}
\label{sec:experiments}
%==============================================================================

The reference configuration is the thermosublimatography setup of
\citet{Liu2025chemical}, used for model assessment by
\citet{Lobresco2026assessment}: a stainless-steel column with an active
section of length $L = \SI{0.7}{m}$ and inner diameter
$D = \SI{4.8}{mm}$, through which dried helium flows at standard volumetric
rates of \SIlist{5;106;540}{mL/min} (\SI{273.15}{K}, \SI{1.01325}{bar}).
A sample of \ce{PbI2} (or of LBE loaded with iodine) is placed in a boat at
the hot end, the evaporation furnace is kept at \SI{1073}{K}, and a gradient
oven imposes a negative axial wall-temperature profile.  After the experiment
the deposition profile along the column is measured by $\gamma$-spectroscopy of
\ce{^{126}I}.
