\begin{abstract}
We present a conservative finite-volume model of vapor transport and wall
 deposition in laminar thermal-gradient columns. Nine Pb--Bi--I gases
 undergo element-conserving equilibrium in a frozen helium carrier and
 exchange material with shared wall inventories. A Hertz--Knudsen--Schrage
 law acts in series with near-wall diffusion. On a Graetz--Robin
 reference problem, this face-flux treatment converges at second order,
 whereas a cell-center sink converges at first order. Fifteen baseline
 and 64 follow-up simulations, supplemented by production and refinement
 studies, distinguish numerical sensitivity from uncertain physical
 inputs. The maximum raw element-balance residue is
 $1.41\times10^{-12}$ of supplied material. Halving the time step or
 adding species correctors changes iodine profiles by at most 0.323\%.
 Upwind and bounded limited-linear advection differ by up to 20.8\% at
 equal mesh. With limited-linear advection, the most sensitive profile
 changes by 1.98\% between 41,472 and 93,312 cells, without a consistent
 observed order. Scaling estimates and upwind sensitivities suggest
 predominantly diffusion-controlled exchange. A $+29\units{kJ\,mol^{-1}}$
 shift in BiI gas Gibbs energy changes the BiI$_3$ share of deposited
 iodide iodine from 94.58\% to 2.01\%. Silica comparisons expose an
 unresolved temperature-coordinate inconsistency. For five published
 PbI$_2$ experiments, wall-only main-deposit onset temperatures have an
 RMS deviation of 18 K, compared with 35 K for two published models.
 This comparison is conditional on the onset region and differing
 carrier, vapor-pressure, and source assumptions; computed peaks remain
 1--4 cm upstream. The evidence establishes conservation and verifies
 the wall discretization, while identifying limitations of predictive
 experimental agreement.
\end{abstract}

\section*{Data Availability}
The solver, the benchmark preparation scripts and the data that support
the findings of this study are openly available in the LESTO repository
at \url{https://github.com/JanKren/LESTO},
\confirm{archived release or DOI, and that the branch holding these
files is public}. These files comprise the completed baseline,
follow-up, production, scheme and mesh, and iteration-check metrics,
the Graetz--Robin verification records, saved axial profiles,
generated tables,
figure-generation code and SHA-256 provenance records. The experimental
curves were digitized from Liu et al.\ \cite{Liu2025chemical}; they
reconstruct published figures, are not author-supplied measurements,
and are adapted under the source paper's CC BY 4.0 license. The
measured profiles, wall temperatures and model curves of Lobresco et
al.\ \cite{Lobresco2026assessment} were digitized in the same way and
are adapted under that paper's CC BY 4.0 license; the case set-up and
final profiles of our comparison runs are included. Some
thermodynamic coefficient files are external inputs that are not
redistributed; their local identifiers and the provenance of the derived
tables are recorded with the benchmark. Reproducing the CFD calculations
from the coefficients requires access to those inputs, whereas
regenerating the figures from the saved results does not.
