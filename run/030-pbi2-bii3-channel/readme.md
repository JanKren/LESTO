# PbI2–BiI3 preliminary channel (M10a)

Two independent gas/condensate pairs share one prescribed-release boat.
The frozen helium carrier is a synthetic 100 × 24 cell plane channel,
0.1 m long, 4.8 mm high and 1 mm thick, with temperature decreasing from
1000 K to 350 K. The mass flux is 0.01743 kg/(m² s), pressure 101325 Pa.
Both gases use a provisional constant diffusivity of 1e-4 m²/s. HKS uses
accommodation = Ce = kineticScale = 1. Neither a GEMS bridge nor gas
re-speciation is used.

The source ratio follows the inferred PbI2/BiI3 deposits of Liu et al.
(2025), LBE-I_SS_II, and is scaled to an iodine mole fraction of 3.9e-6
in this carrier. Release lasts 2 s; the simulation runs to 3 s. Amounts
are in `benchmark.json`. KI, metal vapour and source speciation are excluded
from this qualification case. It is not the paper's geometry or a fitted
experimental prediction; measured T(x), carrier flow reference conditions,
boat geometry and evaporation history are still needed.

Build the solver from `applications/rhoFixedFlowFoam/Allwmake` after loading
OpenFOAM v2412 or v2606. Set `LESTO_M10_DATA` to an external directory holding
`pv_BiI3_phases.csv` generated by `thermochemistry/systems/make_pv_tables.py`.
The PbI2 table comes from the repository. Then run `./Allrun` here. Inputs
are copied into the ignored `work/case`; output goes there. The script
builds the small existing plane-channel carrier helper into `work/bin`.
Restricted Bi thermodynamic records and derived tables are not bundled.

`verification.json` records the preliminary v2412 serial run: 1% onsets
561.16 K (PbI2) and 449.98 K (BiI3), versus independent saturation crossings
567.06 K and 450.38 K. Release totals and pair closures agree within 1e-14
of the supplied masses. These onsets differ from deposit peak temperatures;
`Tpeak` is reported separately in the pair and elemental profile headers.
